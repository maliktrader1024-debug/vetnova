FC FEEDS

Professional Nutrition for Better Farm Performance

Purpose-focused feed solutions designed around dairy, calf and meat-production requirements.

FC 1111 — Extra Milk Feed

Premium dairy feed formulated for high-producing animals, providing nutritional support for milk production, sustained energy, body condition and overall productivity. Designed for farmers focused on consistent performance and a well-managed dairy feeding program.

FC 111 — Extra Milk Feed

A performance-oriented dairy feed designed to support increased milk production, strong nutritional support and consistent animal performance. Its nutritional profile helps provide the energy and nutrients required by productive dairy animals while supporting healthy body condition.

FC 11 — Extra Milk Feed

A balanced dairy nutrition solution developed to support milk production, energy requirements and overall animal performance. Suitable for farmers looking for dependable nutritional support as part of their regular dairy feeding program.

FC 1 — Extra Milk Feed

A practical and reliable dairy feed formulated to support milk production, nutritional balance, healthy body condition and productive performance. Designed to help farmers maintain consistency in everyday dairy operations.

FC 26 — Calf Starter

A specially formulated starter feed for young calves during their early growth stage. Designed to provide nutritional support for healthy growth, development and early rumen development, helping establish a strong foundation for future productivity.

FC 23 — Meat Grow

A specialized feed for growing meat animals, formulated to support healthy weight gain, muscle development, body growth and consistent performance. Designed for farmers focused on achieving efficient growth through a properly managed feeding program.

FC Super Meat Grow — Advanced Meat Growth Formula

An advanced meat-growing feed designed for animals requiring strong nutritional support during the growth and finishing stages. Formulated to support healthy body development, muscle growth, consistent weight gain and efficient feed utilization.

From Calf Starter to Extra Milk and Meat Growth — FC Feeds provides purpose-focused nutrition for every stage of production.
