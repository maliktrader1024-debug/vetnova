Returns, Refunds & Warranty

Manage customer claims with an auditable review process.

Proposed customer policy

  • Show delivery charges and estimated delivery time before checkout.
  • Report damaged, incorrect or incomplete orders promptly; proposed target is within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Eligible unused change-of-mind items may be requested within 7 days, subject to product conditions and applicable law.
  • Defective, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods are reviewed regardless of change-of-mind exclusions.
  • Approved refunds are targeted within 5–7 business days, depending on payment provider/bank processing.
  • Opened feed and consumables are not accepted for change of mind, but valid quality or wrong-item claims must be investigated.
  • Machinery claims follow product-specific inspection, warranty, repair/replacement and refund terms disclosed before purchase.

These are proposed service targets, not a substitute for applicable consumer law or manufacturer warranty obligations. Publish only after legal and operational review.
